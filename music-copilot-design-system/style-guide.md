# Music Copilot Style Guide

Visual system for Music Copilot: a sheet-music page. Manuscript paper, bars outlined in ink, indigo headings and selected chords, green markers for in-scale notes, and amber for notes outside the scale. Rubik for headings and Source Sans 3 for body text.

Files: `scss/colors.scss`, `scss/color-functions.scss`, `scss/tokens.scss`, `scss/effects.scss`, `scss/system.scss`. Start with `scss/index.scss`.

## 4. Contrast

WCAG 2.x ratios. Body text needs 4.5:1. Large display text needs 3:1.

| Use | Text | Background | Ratio | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body text on the manuscript paper | `#1b1b22` | `#f6f1e4` | 15.18 : 1 | AAA body |
| Secondary text on the manuscript paper | `#5f5f6b` | `#f6f1e4` | 5.58 : 1 | AA body |
| Note on a bar | `#5f5f6b` | `#fffdf6` | 6.19 : 1 | AA body |
| Button text on indigo | `#ffffff` | `#3b3f9c` | 8.83 : 1 | AAA body |
| Heading text on a bar | `#3b3f9c` | `#fffdf6` | 8.68 : 1 | AAA body |
| In-scale note text on a bar | `#2e8b57` | `#fffdf6` | 4.17 : 1 | AA large only |
| Out-of-scale note text on a bar (darkened amber) | `#b86e00` | `#fffdf6` | 3.92 : 1 | AA large only |
| Blocked note text on the paper | `#b23a48` | `#f6f1e4` | 5.18 : 1 | AA body |

## 12. Components

### Layout

The page is a sheet of manuscript: cream paper, a header with a treble-clef badge, and stacked bars outlined in ink. Each bar has a sign tag on its top edge. Two-column bars sit side by side on wide screens and stack on phones. Chord buttons sit in a row, and the readout sits under them.

Bars have a 2px ink outline and a 22px radius. Each has a sign tag on its top edge.

Chord buttons are outlined pills. The selected chord turns indigo with white text.

In-scale notes carry a green check. Out-of-scale notes carry an amber mark. Each uses a symbol as well as color.

Suggestions have an indigo left edge and show the reason in the same card.

The staff block is a manuscript panel with a text diagram of a progression or scale.

Primary buttons are indigo pills with white text. Hover lifts 1px. Focus shows a green ring.

The note line appears under every suggestion in the same place.

## 13. Usage rules

- Change base colors in `colors.scss` only. Ladders, states, and contrast update on their own.
- Components use tokens, never raw hex or pixel values.
- Note status is never shown by color alone. Each note also carries a symbol and a text label.
- Body text on any colored background must reach 4.5:1. Check with `ensure-contrast()`.
- Suggestions never change the song. Only an accepted action does.
- Touch targets are at least 44px.
